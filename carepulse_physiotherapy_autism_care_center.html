Frequently Asked Questions

How is the clinic adapted for autism sensory sensitivities?

Our facility features dimmable warm lighting, noise-canceling acoustics, private multi-sensory rooms, and customizable session volume to prevent sensory overload.

Do I need a physician referral for physical therapy sessions?

In most states, direct access allows you to begin initial evaluation and therapy without a referral. We also work directly with pediatricians and orthopedic surgeons.

Can parents observe pediatric or autism sessions?

Absolutely. Parents and caregivers are welcome to observe in our viewing lounge or participate directly in sensory-building activities.
