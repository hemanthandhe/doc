Comprehensive Care

Tailored Therapy Programs

Explore our specialized physical rehabilitation and autism spectrum support services delivered by certified professionals.

Orthopedic & Joint Rehab

Evidence-based therapy targeting joint pain, ligament injuries, arthritis, and musculoskeletal recovery to regain full strength.

  • Post-operative mobility restoration
  • Custom joint mobilization techniques

Sensory Integration Therapy

Designed for children and adults with ASD to process sensory inputs (tactile, auditory, proprioceptive) effectively in calm environments.

  • Customized multi-sensory room sessions
  • Vestibular & body awareness exercises

Pediatric Physical Therapy

Fun and engaging movement therapies aimed at improving motor skills, posture, balance, and physical milestones for children.

  • Developmental milestone acceleration
  • Neuromuscular coordination games

ABA & Behavioral Support

Positive reinforcement framework focused on developing essential daily routines, communication, and emotional regulation skills.

  • Individualized functional behavior plans
  • Adaptive social skill development

Speech & Language Therapy

Targeted communication therapy empowering non-verbal and verbal individuals to express needs, improve articulation, and utilize AAC devices.

  • AAC (Augmentative Communication) support
  • Pragmatic language & conversation skill

Sports & Neurological Rehab

Specialized interventions for neuro-rehabilitation (stroke, cerebral palsy) alongside high-performance athletic injury recovery.

  • Dynamic neuromuscular re-education
  • Electro-stimulation & dry needling options

Our Approach

Step-by-Step Care Journey

How we collaborate with patients, families, and doctors for sustained progress.

01

Sensory Evaluation

Comprehensive initial screening assessing joint range, motor reflexes, sensory thresholds, and behavioral baselines.

02

Custom Care Roadmap

Collaborative strategy formulating SMART milestone targets tailored to individual physical capabilities and comfort zones.

03

Active Integrated Therapy

One-on-one sessions in our specialized gym and multi-sensory sensory rooms combining physio and developmental therapies.

04

Milestones & Home Guidance

Continuous progress tracking, family coaching, and home environment adjustments for long-term independence.
